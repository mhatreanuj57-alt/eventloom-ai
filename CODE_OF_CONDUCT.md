# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

EventLoom AI aims to be a respectful, welcoming collaboration space for people of all experience levels and backgrounds. Contributors should be able to participate without harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project events, and other spaces representing the project.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with patience, dignity, and respect.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Assume missing context before assigning motives; ask for clarification respectfully.
- Respect different cultures, celebrations, identities, abilities, and access needs.
- Credit others' contributions and respect intellectual property.
- Keep private information private and follow reasonable moderator instructions.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, discriminatory slurs, sexual harassment, unwelcome sexual content, impersonation, malicious disclosure of private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Repeated personal attacks, targeted humiliation, retaliation against a reporter, or knowingly submitting harmful or deceptive material may also lead to moderation. Respectful disagreement about design and implementation is welcome.

## Reporting

The repository owner must name a conduct contact and provide a private reporting channel before opening broad community participation. No conduct mailbox or named committee currently exists.

Once a GitHub repository exists, review the owner's profile for an explicitly published private contact. If none is available, a public issue may request a reporting channel **without identifying affected people or describing the incident**. Do not publish private details while waiting for a channel.

If a report concerns the primary moderator, request an alternate reviewer where available. Do not route a conduct report through a security advisory unless it also concerns a security vulnerability. GitHub's platform reporting tools remain available for behavior on GitHub.

## Handling reports

Moderators should acknowledge reports when able, limit details to people needed for review, consider the available context, and avoid promises of absolute confidentiality they cannot keep. Reporters may provide links, dates, descriptions, and their preferred way to be contacted; unnecessary personal details are not required.

The subject of a report should have a reasonable opportunity to respond when doing so does not expose the reporter or worsen the harm. A moderator involved in a dispute should step aside when an independent reviewer is available.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and repetition, maintainers may give a private correction, request changes to content, issue a warning, limit participation, or impose a temporary or permanent ban. Serious threats or harassment can lead directly to a ban.

Moderators should explain the reason and duration of restrictions where appropriate, document significant decisions privately, and apply the policy consistently. Appeals may request review through the same private contact, preferably by an uninvolved moderator.

This policy describes project moderation; it does not promise continuous monitoring or a guaranteed response timeline.
